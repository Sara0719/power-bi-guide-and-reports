How to Use These Reports

📌 Getting Started:
  1. Open the relevant report dashboard
  2. Use filters to customize the time period and segments
  3. Hover over visualizations for detailed tooltips
  4. Click on charts to drill down into detailed data
  5. Export reports as needed for presentations

Tips for Analysis

  • Compare current metrics with historical data
  • Use drill-down features to investigate anomalies
  • Apply filters to isolate specific segments
  • Look for trends and patterns in the data
  • Share insights with stakeholders

Data Refresh Schedule

All reports are automatically refreshed to ensure you have the most current data:

  • Real-time Reports: Updated every hour
  • Daily Reports: Updated at 6:00 AM daily
  • Weekly Reports: Updated every Monday at 8:00 AM
  • Monthly Reports: Updated on the first of each month
